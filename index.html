    I treat AI models not as black-box magic, but as probabilistic compute engines that demand strict deterministic constraints, low latency, and zero-trust security.

    My journey started with low-level systems programming in C++: understanding compiler ASTs, pointer arithmetic, memory layouts, and data structures. When the generative AI wave swept the industry, I saw that the majority of developers were just wrapping external APIs with prompt strings.

    I took a different path. I dug into PyTorch tensor math, fine-tuning 8-billion parameter models with QLoRA and Unsloth, writing custom Ollama Modelfiles for local edge inference, and designing compiler-level SQL firewalls that intercept Prompt-to-SQL attacks before they ever reach a database.

    As the founder of GlitchGo, my primary focus is building our flagship product, AdminZero — an enterprise-grade security gateway that isolates AI agents from sensitive database credentials and deterministically intercepts Prompt-to-SQL attacks at 2.8ms latency. Alongside engineering, through my YouTube channel Tech x Tech, I produce comprehensive Telugu-language technical content demystifying developer tooling, backend systems, and modern AI architectures for thousands of engineers.
